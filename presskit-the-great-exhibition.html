Marketing Copy

Ready-to-use descriptions, free to quote in whole or in part.

One-liner

The Great Exhibition is a museum-curator adventure: raid the tombs of long-lost civilizations, fund daring expeditions, arrange prestigious displays - and unravel the mystery of Silas Vane.

Short description

The Great Exhibition is an adventure game about curating a museum. Venture into the temples of long-lost civilizations to recover ancient artifacts, dodge lethal traps and solve puzzles, then bring your finds home: arrange prestigious displays, complete artifact sets, fund new expeditions, and uncover the mystery of Silas Vane.

Long description

The Great Exhibition is an adventure game about two worlds: the dark, trap-riddled temples of civilizations the world forgot, and the sunlit halls of the museum you run. In the field, you'll descend into long-forgotten tombs, avoid lethal traps, solve puzzles, and search for artifacts worthy of the collection. Back home, you'll arrange displays for prestige, gather artifact sets to raise their value, and attract the attention of world-renowned explorers.

Buy specialized equipment, finance expeditions to unexplored lands, and piece together the stories behind the civilizations you unearth. The tombs are connected in more ways than they first appear - and every artifact points toward a larger mystery, and a man named Silas Vane.

Key features

  • Explore long-forgotten temples: avoid lethal traps, solve puzzles, and recover ancient artifacts.
  • Curate your museum: arrange prestigious displays and complete artifact sets to raise their value.
  • Fund expeditions to unexplored lands and buy specialized equipment.
  • Attract world-renowned explorers as your museum's reputation grows.
  • Interconnected tombs and a larger mystery: the story of Silas Vane.

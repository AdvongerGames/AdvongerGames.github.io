Marketing Copy

Ready-to-use descriptions, free to quote in whole or in part.

One-liner

Realms of Madness is a fantasy castlebuilder RTS where you build a medieval castle, command mythical forces across sea, land, and sky, and demolish the enemy stronghold.

Short description

Realms of Madness is a fantasy castlebuilder RTS. Build a medieval fantasy castle, expand your economy and upgrades, then lead mythical sea, ground, and sky forces against the enemy stronghold - with a fully voice-acted campaign cast, an original soundtrack, and dragons waiting in the late game.

Long description

Realms of Madness is a fantasy castlebuilder RTS fought on a layered battlefield. Raise a medieval stronghold, grow its economy, and unlock upgrades and chaotic abilities - then send your forces out across the water, over the land, and into the sky. Naval assaults, ground sieges, and airborne attacks all unfold at once, and victory means reading every layer of the fight while your own walls hold.

The campaign carries you through the realms of a mad world, guided by a fully voice-acted cast of scribes, kings, and stranger things, set to an original soundtrack. Build your fortress, master the madness, and - when the late game arrives - bring dragons to the battle and tear the enemy castle down.

Key features

  • Build a medieval fantasy castle and expand its economy and upgrades.
  • Command sea, ground, and sky forces on a layered battlefield.
  • Unlock chaotic abilities and bring dragons into the late-game battle.
  • Fully voice-acted campaign characters and an original soundtrack.
  • Demolish the enemy stronghold across the realms of a mad world.

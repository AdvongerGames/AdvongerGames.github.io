Marketing Copy

One-liner

Open The Gates! is a sidescrolling castlebuilder RTS about building, managing, defending, and attacking medieval castles across a 26+ level single-player campaign.

Short description

Open The Gates! is a sidescrolling castlebuilder RTS. Construct houses and resource buildings, keep your citizens happy, and fortify your walls with towers and traps. Survive enemy sieges, then counterattack with units and powerful siege weapons across a fully voice-acted campaign of more than 26 levels.

Long description

Open The Gates! is a sidescrolling castlebuilder RTS where every castle is both a home and a fortress. Build houses and resource buildings, manage your economy, and keep your citizens fed and happy - because when the enemy army appears on the horizon, everything you've built is what stands between them and the keep.

Raise walls, place towers and traps, and command your units through desperate defenses. Then take the fight back: march on enemy fortresses with soldiers and siege weapons and tear their gates down. A linear single-player campaign of more than 26 levels - plus extra siege mode levels - is carried by a fully voice-acted cast of nobles, jesters, thieves, and monsters, each with their own part to play in the kingdom's story.

Key features

  • Build and manage a living medieval castle: houses, resource buildings, and citizens with needs.
  • Defend your walls with units, towers, and traps - then counterattack with siege weapons.
  • Linear single-player campaign with more than 26 levels, plus extra siege mode levels.
  • A fully voice-acted cast of campaign characters.
  • Free demo available on Steam.
